# AminoVITAL — HSA Text & Claims Compliance (MANDATORY)

Source: HSA *Health Supplements Guidelines* (GL-CHPB-3-001, Jan 2026) +
*Guidelines for Claims and Claims Substantiation of HS and TM* (GL-CHPB-1-005, Jan 2025).

AminoVITAL is a **Health Supplement (HS)** — amino acid sports nutrition. ANY text rendered
into an AminoVITAL image (headlines, sublines, callouts, captions baked into the image) MUST
comply with the rules below. When in doubt, choose the more conservative wording.

---

## ALLOWED — safe building blocks

**Permitted action verbs** (use these): aid, assist, help, maintain, support, contribute to,
nourish, supplement, replenish, fuel, provide, decrease, reduce, relieve, increase, enhance,
improve, promote.

**Permitted targets / claims for amino-acid sports nutrition:**
- "amino acid nutrition" / "amino acids" / "essential amino acids (EAA)" / "BCAA"
- "support energy production" / "steady fuel" / "energy for the long haul"
- "maintain / support body electrolyte balance"
- "help relieve / reduce fatigue and tiredness"
- "support the body before / during / after exercise" (timing framing is fine)
- "part of a training nutrition routine" / "pre-training nutrition" / "post-training nutrition"
- "support general health and wellbeing" / "maintain good health"
- "contribute to normal muscle function" (acceptable wording — do NOT escalate to "repair")
- Factual nutritional figures: kcal, amino acid mg, BCAA mg, serving timing, "no water needed"

**Permitted qualifiers:** mild, temporarily, short-term, healthy adults, athletes, during/after
intense physical exercise, general.

---

## PROHIBITED — never render any of these

1. **Disease treatment / prevention** of ANY condition (HSA Table 6), e.g. diabetes, hypertension,
   cancer, arthritis, osteoporosis, cholesterol, depression, obesity, etc. No "treat", "cure",
   "prevent", "heal" tied to a disease/condition.
2. **Recovery / muscle-damage repair claims** that imply healing: NO "recover faster",
   "less sore", "reduces muscle damage", "repairs muscles", "speeds recovery", "fixes soreness".
   (Safe alternative: "part of a post-training nutrition routine", "amino acid nutrition after training".)
3. **"Clinically Proven" / "Proven by Clinical Trials"** or any implied disease-efficacy proof.
4. **Anti-aging** — no "reverse aging", "anti-ageing", "turn back the clock".
5. **Hormonal claims** — no "boost testosterone", "balance hormones", etc.
6. **Blood sugar / glucose management** — no "lower blood sugar", no insulin/HbA1C terms,
   no reference to diabetic / pre-diabetic use.
7. **Sexual function** — no virility / libido / performance claims.
8. **Stress-prevention** — no "beats the stress of modern life". (Allowed: "nutritional support".)
9. **Study / cognition performance** — no "makes you smarter", "improves IQ", "boosts focus for exams".
10. **Superlatives & absolutes** — no "best", "#1", "the only", "miraculous", "magical",
    "100% safe", "no side effects", "guaranteed".
11. **Endorsement / testimonial framing** — no "doctors recommend", no healthcare-professional or
    celebrity endorsement, no first-person efficacy testimonial ("this helps me recover").
12. **Substituting diet / medication** — must not imply it replaces a balanced diet or prescribed meds.
13. **Exploiting fear** — no fear-based or superstition-based messaging.
14. **Jargon** — no "pharmaceutical grade", "nanoclusters", or confusing scientific terms.
15. **HSA logos / names** — never depict HSA marks.

---

## Applying this to image overlay text

- Keep headlines factual or function-supporting, never disease/efficacy.
- Numbers (kcal, mg amino acids, BCAA mg, timing) are encouraged — they are factual and compliant.
- Timing language ("take 30 min before exercise", "within 30 min after training") is compliant.
- If a requested headline edges toward a prohibited claim, rewrite it to the nearest compliant
  phrasing and note the change in the prompt (the image must only contain the compliant version).
- Spelling of rendered text must be EXACT — image models distort text, so specify each string in
  quotes and instruct the model to reproduce it character-for-character.
