# Food Content — Prompt Template (gpt-image-2)

Build ONE flowing English prompt of **120–250 words** following the block order
below. The blocks are your reasoning scaffold — do NOT print headings into the
prompt. Open with intent, not praise. If a branded product appears, its photo is
passed as reference #1 — **preserve it, don't re-describe the label**.

> Read `modes/_shared.md` first. Hex only for brand/accent colors. No magic
> words. One light direction. One style anchor.

---

### 1 · Intent (opening clause)
- `Create a warm editorial food photograph for an Instagram post of …`
- `Create an appetizing overhead food shot of …`

### 2 · Subject — the dish (2–3 sentences, the heart of the prompt)
Name the dish with full specificity: cuisine + regional variant + dish name
(`authentic Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen`, not `a bowl of ramen`). Then describe
the **visible components that define it** — key toppings (with quantity), the
vessel, and one or two appetite cues (steam, gloss, char). Be concrete but don't
catalogue every grain. Hex codes only where a color is genuinely important
(e.g. a yolk gradient), not for everything.

### 3 · Branded product (1 line, only if present — do NOT describe the label)
> "Include the <SKU name> from reference image #1, <where in the scene>. Preserve
> its packaging exactly as in reference #1; do not alter any label text."

### 4 · Scene & background (1–2 sentences)
Surface (one material + color) and a simple background with blur, e.g.
`on a dark-stained oak table; warm out-of-focus restaurant interior behind at
roughly f/2.8`. Keep props to 2–4, each named briefly with position.

### 5 · Key details (composition + lighting)
- **Composition:** subject placement + negative space (e.g. `dish on the
  upper-right third, breathing room lower-left`).
- **Lighting:** ONE key direction + ONE color temperature + optional rim, e.g.
  `soft key from upper-left ~5000K; gentle warm backlight catching the steam`.

### 6 · Text
Usually `none`. If overlay text is requested, quote it exactly with position.

### 7 · Style anchor + constraints
- Style: one reference, e.g. `warm editorial food photography, appetizing and
  homely`.
- Constraints (one sentence): `no text; no human hands or faces; no extra logos;
  no plastic-looking or artificial-looking food`.

---

### Logo (only if the channel bakes one into food posts)
> "Place the brand logo from reference image #2 in the <corner>, ~<N>% of canvas
> width, ~<N>% padding; <color rule>; preserve glyph shapes, do not distort."

### Worked example (~150 words)
> *Create a warm editorial food photograph for an Instagram post of authentic
> Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen. The bowl holds rich, opaque ivory-white pork-bone
> broth with two slices of caramelized chashu, a soft-boiled ajitama egg halved to
> show a jammy orange yolk, a fold of nori, and a scatter of green scallion; fine
> wisps of steam rise from the surface. The bowl sits on a dark-stained oak table,
> with a warm out-of-focus restaurant interior behind at roughly f/2.8 and a pair
> of walnut chopsticks resting to the right. Composition: bowl on the upper-right
> third with breathing room in the lower-left. Lighting: soft key from the
> upper-left around 5000K, with a gentle warm backlight at 3500K catching the
> rising steam and the back rim of the bowl. Style: warm editorial food
> photography, appetizing and homely. No text; no human hands or faces; no extra
> logos; no plastic-looking or artificial-looking food.*
