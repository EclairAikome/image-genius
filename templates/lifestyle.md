# Lifestyle Content — Prompt Template (gpt-image-2)

Build ONE flowing English prompt of **120–250 words** following the block order
below. The blocks are your reasoning scaffold — do NOT print headings into the
prompt. Open with intent, not praise. If a branded product appears, its photo is
passed as reference #1 — **preserve it, don't re-describe the label**.

> Read `modes/_shared.md` first. Hex only for brand/accent colors. No magic
> words. One light direction. One style anchor. No identifiable faces.

---

### 1 · Intent (opening clause)
- `Create a calm lifestyle photograph for an Instagram post showing …`
- `Create an energetic post-workout lifestyle scene of …`

### 2 · Scene & activity (2–3 sentences)
Describe the activity through hands, objects, and environment — **no
identifiable faces**. Show objects in use to imply life (a cup mid-pour, a mat
mid-roll). Name the few elements that carry the story, with position; hex only
for brand-relevant colors.

### 3 · Branded product integration (1 line, only if present)
> "Include the <SKU name> from reference image #1, sitting naturally <where>.
> Preserve its packaging exactly as in reference #1; do not alter any label text."

### 4 · Environment & key details (1–2 sentences)
The setting (architecture, light source, a couple of furnishings or plants) and
composition: subject zone + where the negative space goes + a sense of depth
(foreground / midground / background).

### 5 · Lighting (1 clause)
ONE key direction + ONE color temperature, usually natural, e.g. `soft natural
window light from the right, ~5500K, gentle fill from bright walls`.

### 6 · Text
Usually `none`. If overlay text is requested, quote it exactly with position.
(AminoVITAL: any on-image claim must be HSA-compliant.)

### 7 · Style anchor + constraints
- Style: one reference, e.g. `calm wellness editorial, Scandinavian-meets-Japanese`
  or `bright energetic sports-lifestyle`.
- Constraints (one sentence): `no identifiable faces; no text; no extra logos;
  no cluttered background; no staged stock-photo look`.

---

### Logo (when the channel bakes one in)
> "Place the brand logo from reference image #2 in the <corner>, ~<N>% of canvas
> width, ~<N>% padding; <color rule>; preserve glyph shapes, do not distort."

---

### Worked example (~150 words)
> *Create a calm lifestyle photograph for an Instagram post showing a quiet
> morning self-care moment. A pair of hands rolls out a sage-green yoga mat
> (#8FBC8F) diagonally across light oak flooring; a ceramic cup of matcha sits at
> the upper-right beside a folded linen throw. The setting is a bright modern
> apartment with floor-to-ceiling windows and a monstera plant framing the left
> edge; the activity zone sits on the right third with airy negative space on the
> left, and layered depth from the foreground plant to the window behind. Lighting:
> soft natural window light from the right at about 5500K, with gentle fill bouncing
> off the pale walls. Style: calm wellness editorial, Scandinavian-meets-Japanese,
> lifted and airy. No identifiable faces; no text; no extra logos; no cluttered
> background; no staged stock-photo look.*
