# Lifestyle Content — Prompt Template

## Template Structure

Generate the prompt by filling each section below IN ORDER. Do not skip any section.
Separate sections with periods. The final prompt should read as one cohesive paragraph.

### Section 1: Shot Type & Photography Style
```
Professional lifestyle photography, {shot_type}, {lens_description}
```

**shot_type** options:
- `wide establishing shot` — for scene-setting, room interiors, outdoor spaces
- `medium shot with environmental context` — for activity-focused scenes
- `close-up detail shot` — for textures, accessories, small moments
- `overhead bird's-eye view` — for flat lays, desk setups, yoga mats

**lens_description** options:
- `35mm lens, f/4, natural perspective` — versatile lifestyle standard
- `85mm lens, f/1.8, creamy bokeh` — for isolated subjects
- `24mm lens, wide angle, expansive feel` — for spaces and environments

### Section 2: Main Subject
```
{detailed_scene_description}
```

Rules:
- Describe the scene or activity, not people's faces
- Focus on hands, objects, environments, and activities
- Include 3-5 specific visual details
- Use sensory language (sun-drenched, breezy, crisp, etc.)
- Example: "A sun-drenched morning yoga setup on a minimalist wooden deck, with a sage green yoga mat unrolled, a ceramic cup of matcha tea with visible foam, and a small potted succulent"

### Section 3: Setting & Background
```
{environment_description}
```

Options:
- `In a bright, airy modern apartment with large windows letting in natural light and minimal Scandinavian decor`
- `On an outdoor terrace surrounded by lush green plants and warm morning sunlight`
- `In a cozy cafe corner with exposed brick walls and warm pendant lighting`
- `At a clean, organized workspace with natural wood tones and plants`
- `In a serene outdoor garden setting with dappled sunlight through trees`

### Section 4: Composition & Framing
```
{composition_description}
```

Options:
- `Balanced composition with leading lines drawing the eye to the main subject`
- `Rule of thirds with the key element placed at the intersection point, generous negative space`
- `Layered composition with foreground interest, midground subject, and background context`
- `Centered symmetrical composition for a calm, balanced feel`

### Section 5: Lighting
```
{lighting_description}
```

Options:
- `Soft, diffused natural daylight creating a warm, golden hour glow with long gentle shadows`
- `Bright, even midday light with minimal shadows for a fresh, clean aesthetic`
- `Warm backlit scene with sun flares and a dreamy, ethereal quality`
- `Cozy indoor ambient lighting mixed with window light for a hygge atmosphere`

### Section 6: Color Palette
```
{color_description}
```

- `Soft, muted earth tones — warm beige, sage green, terracotta, and cream`
- `Clean and airy — whites, light grays, pale blues, and natural wood tones`
- `Warm and vibrant — golden yellows, burnt orange, deep greens`
- `Cool and calming — soft blues, lavender, mint, and white`

Incorporate brand colors from config as accent tones.

### Section 7: Supporting Elements & Props
```
{props_description}
```

Pick 2-4 relevant lifestyle props:
- Wellness: yoga mat, water bottle, journal, candle, essential oils
- Home: plants, books, ceramics, linen throws, woven baskets
- Productivity: notebook, pen, laptop (closed), coffee cup, glasses
- Outdoor: sun hat, tote bag, picnic blanket, fresh flowers

### Section 8: Atmosphere & Mood
```
{mood_description}
```

- `Calm, mindful, and intentional — a moment of quiet self-care`
- `Energetic and fresh — the start of an active, productive day`
- `Cozy and comfortable — warmth, relaxation, and simple pleasures`
- `Aspirational yet approachable — elevated everyday living`
