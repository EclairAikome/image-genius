# Product Content — Prompt Template

## Template Structure

Generate the prompt by filling each section below IN ORDER. Do not skip any section.
Separate sections with periods. The final prompt should read as one cohesive paragraph.

### Section 1: Shot Type & Photography Style
```
Professional commercial product photography, {shot_type}, {lens_description}
```

**shot_type** options:
- `hero shot on a clean surface` — single product showcase
- `lifestyle product shot in context` — product in use/environment
- `flat lay arrangement` — multiple items organized from above
- `detail macro shot` — close-up of product features/textures

**lens_description** options:
- `100mm macro lens, f/5.6, razor-sharp detail` — for product detail
- `50mm lens, f/4, clean and neutral` — standard product shot
- `85mm lens, f/2.0, subject isolation with smooth bokeh` — hero product

### Section 2: Main Subject
```
{detailed_product_description}
```

Rules:
- Describe the product's physical appearance precisely
- Include materials, textures, colors, and finish
- Mention size/scale context if relevant
- Note any key features visible in the shot
- Example: "A sleek matte black ceramic pour-over coffee dripper with a natural cork collar, sitting on a clear glass server filled with freshly brewed amber coffee, steam gently rising"

### Section 3: Setting & Background
```
{surface_and_background}
```

Options:
- `On a seamless white background with subtle shadow for depth`
- `On a natural stone surface with a softly blurred neutral background`
- `On a clean wooden surface with a gradient background matching the brand palette`
- `In its natural use environment with context-appropriate surroundings`

### Section 4: Composition & Framing
```
{composition_description}
```

Options:
- `Centered hero placement with ample breathing room and clean negative space`
- `Rule of thirds with supporting props creating visual balance`
- `Dynamic angle slightly from below, giving the product presence and stature`
- `Straight-on product view with perfect symmetry`

### Section 5: Lighting
```
{lighting_description}
```

Options:
- `Professional three-point studio lighting — key light from 45 degrees, fill light opposite, rim light for edge separation`
- `Soft, diffused single light source creating gentle gradients and minimal shadows`
- `Natural window light with a white bounce card for shadow fill, clean and authentic`
- `Dramatic spotlight with dark background for premium, luxury feel`

### Section 6: Color Palette
```
{color_description}
```

- `Clean, neutral palette — whites, light grays, and the product's natural colors as the focal point`
- `Warm complementary tones that make the product colors pop without competing`
- `Dark, moody tones — deep charcoals and blacks for a luxury premium feel`
- `Brand-aligned palette using {brand_colors} as background or accent tones`

### Section 7: Supporting Elements & Props
```
{props_description}
```

Pick 1-3 minimal props that contextualize the product:
- Usage items that show the product in action
- Raw materials or ingredients if it's a consumable
- Complementary products from the same brand family
- Simple geometric shapes or textures for visual interest

### Section 8: Atmosphere & Mood
```
{mood_description}
```

- `Clean, premium, and aspirational — the product speaks for itself`
- `Warm and approachable — accessible luxury, everyday premium`
- `Bold and modern — confident, innovative, forward-thinking`
- `Natural and authentic — honest, sustainable, craft-quality`
